**A No-Code Digital Transaction & Operational Management Platform for Goods and Services**

**VV-PUNYE Open Source** is an open-source software engineered as a no-code **Digital Public Good (DPG)**. It empowers micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs), independent freelancers, and community initiatives to streamline daily operations, eliminate financial intermediary commissions, maintain verifiable transaction records, and ensure complete financial transparency completely free of charge.

The system directly advances **SDG 8 (Decent Work & Economic Growth)** and **SDG 9 (Industry, Innovation, & Infrastructure)** by unifying physical goods inventory, professional services booking, and transparent community treasury tracking into an autonomous, sovereign platform.

## Background & Problem Statement

In today's digital economy, possessing an independent transaction platform and operational recording system is essential for small business owners and independent workers. However, most individuals face steep systemic barriers:

1. **Technical Barriers (Lack of Programming Skills)**  
   Most grassroots entrepreneurs lack technical or software engineering backgrounds. Conventional open-source solutions remain inaccessible due to complicated server setups, deployment scripts, and terminal management.

2. **Subscription Traps & Fee Exploitation**  
   Commercial platforms impose recurring monthly/annual subscription fees or take heavy percentage cuts from every transaction, significantly eroding thin profit margins.

3. **Severe Capital Constraints in Early Stages**  
   For emerging micro-enterprises, paying recurring software rent is an unsustainable burden. When sales fluctuate, digital operations risk immediate termination due to missed subscription payments.

4. **Tooling Fragmentation between Goods and Services**  
   Entrepreneurs offering both physical merchandise and hourly freelance services are forced to juggle multiple disjointed tools, creating operational chaos and wasted productivity.

5. **Loss of Data Sovereignty & Vendor Lock-in**  
   Reliance on centralized third-party platforms strips business owners of true ownership over customer databases, sales histories, and digital assets.

6. **Feature Bloat Overwhelming Beginners**  
   Most commercial enterprise tools are cluttered with excessive menus and complex configurations that confuse non-technical users, discouraging adoption.

7. **Hardware & Connectivity Constraints**  
   Modern business applications often demand high-end specifications and fast internet access, excluding rural and developing communities with limited infrastructure.

8. **Commercial Exploitation of Micro-Merchant Data**  
   Centralized commercial providers routinely monetize and exploit aggregate sales data for internal commercial interests, directly competing against the very merchants using their tools.

9. **Language & Developer-Centric Jargon Barriers**  
   Many open-source tools are written by developers for developers, filled with technical jargon that creates an unnecessary cognitive barrier for local grassroots communities.

## Core Solutions & Key Features

**VV-PUNYE Open Source** acts as a Digital Public Good engineered to remove technical, financial, and infrastructure hurdles through the following core features:

1. **Flexible & Inclusive Operational Modes (Product, Service, Community, or Hybrid)**  
   Features operate on an opt-in basis without rigid structural enforcement. Users activate only the capabilities they need:
   * **Product & Inventory Mode**: Physical item catalog, daily stock tracking, and point-of-sale checkout.
   * **Service & Portfolio Mode**: Personal skill presentation, professional service listings, equipment rentals, and appointment scheduling.
   * **Community & Social Initiative Mode**: Public treasury tracking, transparent activity funding, and civic initiative management.
   * **Seamless Hybrid Mode**: Unrestricted freedom to blend product sales, service bookings, and community accounting within a unified workspace.

2. **Transparent & Globally Standardized Financial Recording (Investor-Ready)**  
   Daily tracking is intuitive for beginners, yet structured to scale toward international accountability:
   * **Cross-Device Usability**: Fully responsive on smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers.
   * **International Financial Standard Readiness**: Supports multi-currency operations and structured global accounting ledgers to assist local businesses in attracting international grants and investments.
   * **Transparency & Accountability**: Generates verifiable cash flow summaries, profit/loss statements, and balance sheets ready for stakeholders.
   * **Intuitive Financial Health Dashboard**: Clear visual indicators that communicate business health without complex accounting jargon.
   * **Open Data Export**: Complete transaction histories can be exported at any time to open formats (CSV, JSON, PDF).

3. **Lightweight, Modular Architecture & Seamless Upgrades**  
   Maintains core functions and optional add-ons in clean isolation to guarantee stability and agility:
   * **Ultra-Fast & Resource-Efficient Core**: Highly optimized codebase ensuring smooth execution on older PCs and entry-level hardware without latency.
   * **One-Click Feature Toggles**: Advanced capabilities (such as automated receipt parsing or analytics modules) can be enabled via clean interface switches without complex reconfigurations.
   * **System Isolation & Operational Safety**: Modular boundaries ensure that future updates never corrupt legacy database records or halt basic transactional features.
   * **Long-Term Scalability**: Clean architectural boundaries provide a robust foundation for long-term open-source community contributions.

4. **Multi-Deployment Flexibility & Complete Data Sovereignty**  
   Users retain 100% control over their database without vendor dependency (*no vendor lock-in*):
   * **Home / Office Server**: Repurpose existing home PCs or laptops as dedicated servers at zero hardware cost.
   * **Single-Board Computers (SBC)**: Fully compatible with ultra-low-power hardware (Raspberry Pi, Orange Pi, and similar SBCs).
   * **Self-Hosted VPS**: Direct deployment to personal Virtual Private Servers for 24/7 internet availability under sovereign administrator control.
   * **Cloud Provider of Choice**: Effortless deployment across any modern cloud hosting environment.

5. **100% Free Access & Zero Intermediary Fees**  
   Published strictly under an open license to ensure uncompromised economic empowerment:
   * **No Subscriptions & No Feature Lock**: Zero recurring fees, no trial periods, and no paywalls. All modules are unlocked from day one.
   * **Zero Transaction Cuts**: 100% of sales revenue remains with the business owner or freelancer without percentage deductions.
   * **Operational Cost Efficiency**: Eliminates digital overhead entirely, safeguarding business viability during market downturns.
   * **Financial Sovereignty**: Operational continuity can never be suspended by an external commercial vendor.

6. **Lightweight Performance & Internationalization Readiness (i18n)**  
   Engineered for global accessibility across technical and linguistic divides:
   * **Zero-Learning Curve Interface**: Clear visual design that allows beginners to begin operations within minutes.
   * **Universal Screen Adaptation**: Responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports with minimal memory footprint.
   * **Global i18n Architecture**: Clean separation between interface copy and logic to enable community-driven translations.

7. **Privacy-First Architecture & Zero Telemetry**  
   Guarantees maximum data privacy and protection:
   * **No Tracking & No Ads**: The application contains zero third-party analytics trackers, behavioral cookies, or commercial advertisements.
   * **Local Encrypted Storage**: All core operational data is stored and encrypted directly on user-controlled hardware.

8. **Open Standards & Interoperability**  
   Eliminates data silos:
   * **Standardized Formats**: All data structures utilize open formats (JSON, CSV, REST API, Protocol Buffers) for frictionless migration and external integration.
   * **Ecosystem Compatibility**: Readily interfaces with open-source external tools, physical POS hardware, and custom reporting pipelines.

9. **Inclusive Accessibility & Offline-First Design**  
   Ensures mission-critical reliability regardless of network stability:
   * **Offline-First Resilience**: Transactions and inventory updates continue unabated during network blackouts, syncing automatically when connectivity resumes.
   * **Adaptive High-Contrast UI**: Crisp visual hierarchy legible under direct sunlight and accessible to users of diverse digital literacy levels.

## System Architecture & Technology Stack

* **Frontend, Single-Page Application (SPA), & PWA**:
  * **React + Vite (SPA)**: Provides instantaneous route transitions without browser reloads, combined with Vite's rapid build cycles and minimal bundle footprint.
  * **Progressive Web App (PWA)**: Installable directly onto mobile devices (Android/iOS) and desktop operating systems as a native-like app, functioning offline-first without commercial app store dependencies.

* **Data Serialization & Offline Storage**:
  * **Protocol Buffers (Protobuf / .proto)**: Compact open binary schema for data serialization, drastically reducing payload sizes for low-bandwidth networks.
  * **IndexedDB & SQLite**: Royalty-free storage pairing. IndexedDB handles local client-side transaction caching, while SQLite handles server-side persistence.

* **Core Services & Server Engine**:
  * **Fastify (Node.js)**: High-performance backend engine selected for rapid throughput, built-in schema validation, low memory consumption, and rock-solid execution on low-spec single-board computers (SBC).
  * **Open API Architecture**: Clean REST and Protobuf contracts facilitating modular integration with external open systems.

* **Multi-Layer Security & Absolute Privacy**:
  * **Layered Cryptography (AES-256-GCM / WebCrypto API)**: Open cryptographic primitives protecting data at rest on local drives and in transit across network hops. Encryption keys remain strictly under user ownership.
  * **Zero Telemetry**: Completely devoid of analytics beacons, third-party marketing tags, and commercial trackers.

## Multi-Layer Security, Privacy, & Legal Compliance

* **Zero-Telemetry Assurance**: The application collects zero personal identifiers, behavioral metrics, or transaction payloads.
* **Client-Side AES-256-GCM Encryption**: Databases are secured using modern authenticated encryption without backdoor access.
* **Regulatory Compliance (UU PDP & GDPR)**:
  * Adheres strictly to the *Data Minimization* principles of Indonesia Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection (**UU PDP**).
  * Conforms to the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (**GDPR EU 2016/679**).
  * Guarantees inherent support for user data deletion (*Right to be Forgotten*) and data portability.
* **Strict Schema Validation at Ingestion Gate**: Fastify and Protobuf enforce strict compile-time and runtime input parsing to neutralize SQL Injection, Cross-Site Scripting (XSS), and Prototype Pollution vectors.

## Infrastructure Strategy & Network Sovereignty

Engineered with a platform-agnostic philosophy to eliminate cloud provider lock-in:

* **Priority 1 — 100% Open-Source Self-Hosted**: Baseline configurations leverage open reverse-proxies (Caddy, Nginx, or Traefik) and open tunneling protocols (Rathole, BoringProxy, or FRP) for self-sovereign internet exposure.
* **Priority 2 — Optional Protective Services (Opt-In Cloudflare Tunnel/Proxy)**: Documentation provides optional guides for automated DDoS mitigation and managed SSL certificates without coupling application logic to proprietary APIs.
* **Priority 3 — Hardware & Cloud Independence**: Freedom to deploy on recycled personal computers, legacy laptops, Single-Board Computers (Raspberry Pi / Orange Pi), independent VPS instances, or major public clouds.

## Quick Start Guide

A concise guide to provisioning and running the system in a local or staging environment:

### System Prerequisites
Ensure your workstation meets the following runtime requirements:
* **Node.js**: Version `20.x LTS` or higher (Fully tested and verified on Node.js `v22.x` and `v24.x`)
* **NPM**: Version `10.x` or `11.x` or higher
* **Git**: Installed and configured on your operating system

---

### Installation Steps

1. **Clone the Repository & Enter Directory**

   Run the following commands in your terminal:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/vytravyctoria/VV-PUNYE-Open-Source.git
   cd VV-PUNYE-Open-Source
   ```

2. **Install Project Dependencies**

   Install all required runtime libraries:
   ```bash
   npm install
   ```

3. **Configure Environment Variables**

   Duplicate the template configuration into an active environment file:
   * **Linux / macOS / Git Bash**:
     ```bash
     cp .env.example .env
     ```
   * **Windows PowerShell**:
     ```powershell
     Copy-Item .env.example .env
     ```

4. **Launch Development Mode**

   Start the local development servers:
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```
   The client interface (React + Vite) and core API server (Fastify) will launch concurrently and become accessible directly via your web browser.

5. **Run Automated Test Suite (Optional)**

   To verify unit functionality and schema validation integrity:
   ```bash
   npm test
   ```

## Contributing Guide

Contributions from the global community—including bug fixes, architectural enhancements, localization, and feature suggestions—are warmly welcomed.

To preserve repository integrity and protect our user base, please observe the following contribution etiquette:

### 1. Issue Etiquette

Before creating a new ticket on the **Issues** tab, verify that a duplicate issue does not already exist:

* **Bug Reports**:  
  When reporting reproducible bugs or technical errors, select the **Bug Report** template. Please include:
  * A clear summary and sequential steps to reproduce the issue.
  * System specifications (Node.js version, OS/browser, deployment target).
  * Relevant console logs or screenshots.

* **Feature Requests**:  
  When proposing architectural additions or UI improvements, use the **Feature Request** template. Please explain:
  * The practical problem faced by micro-merchants or independent workers in the field.
  * The proposed solution and its expected benefits.
  * *Notice:* Features must align strictly with DPG principles, zero-telemetry, and zero vendor lock-in.

* **Security & Responsible Disclosure — CRITICAL**:  
  If you discover a security vulnerability, cryptographic weakness, or potential data leak:
  * ❌ **DO NOT** submit a public issue on the GitHub board.
  * 🔒 **USE PRIVATE CHANNELS**: Submit your report confidentially through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/vytravyctoria/VV-PUNYE-Open-Source/security/advisories) on this repository. The maintainers will triage and patch the vulnerability before public disclosure.

---

### 2. Code Standards

All technical pull requests must comply with the following principles:
* **Schema Validation**: Every newly introduced API endpoint must be strictly parsed via Fastify and Protobuf schemas.
* **Zero Telemetry**: No third-party tracking beacons, commercial analytics libraries, or hidden cookies are permitted.
* **Dependency Independence**: Proprietary, closed-source, or paywalled dependencies are prohibited.
* **Modularity**: New additions must preserve the core offline-first architecture.

---

### 3. Pull Request (PR) Workflow

1. Create a dedicated feature branch from `main` (`git checkout -b feature/issue-title`).
2. Write concise, descriptive commit messages.
3. Run the automated test suite locally (`npm test`) to prevent regressions.
4. Submit a Pull Request to the `main` branch referencing the corresponding Issue ticket.
